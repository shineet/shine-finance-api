MyFinance

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026. MyFinance is made by Shine Thankappan ("we"). Contact: 2020shine@gmail.com

What MyFinance does

MyFinance shows you your own finances: balances, transactions, debts, investments and plans, from the bank accounts you choose to connect. It is read-only. It cannot move money or make payments.

What we collect

  • Your account: your Sign in with Apple identifier, and your email address if you choose to share it with us.
  • Financial data you connect through Plaid: account names and balances, transactions, credit card terms (rates, minimum payments, due dates), and investment holdings and transactions.
  • Things you enter: goals, card rates, manual assets and app settings.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.

We never see or store your bank username or password. You enter those only into Plaid, our bank connection provider.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to show you your own finances and to send the notifications you turn on. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for anyone else.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run MyFinance, and only for that purpose:

  • Plaid connects your bank and supplies your financial data. Plaid's own privacy policy is at plaid.com/legal.
  • Vercel hosts our server, in the United States.
  • Supabase hosts our database, in the United States.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and push notifications. Notifications never contain amounts or balances.

We will disclose data if the law requires it.

How we protect it

All connections use HTTPS. Data is encrypted at rest, and bank connection tokens are encrypted again with a key only our server holds. Each person's data is kept separate, so no user can see another's. On your device, the app can require Face ID or Touch ID.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. In the app you can disconnect any bank, or delete your account under Settings > Account. Deleting your account disconnects every bank from Plaid and permanently deletes your data from our database. Signing out removes your data from that device. You can also ask us to delete it by emailing the address above; we complete requests within 30 days.

Your choices

You can see all of your data in the app, disconnect banks at any time, turn notifications off, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your data. Email us to use them.

Children

MyFinance is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
