Call Aura from anywhere

Say “Hey Aura” from any app or the Home Screen and MyFinance opens with Aura listening. Your iPhone does the listening, with Apple’s Vocal Shortcuts.

Add the Hey Aura shortcut

Open this page on your iPhone, with MyFinance installed. If Safari saves it as a download, tap the download arrow in the address bar, then the file, then Add Shortcut.

Then teach your iPhone the phrase

  1. Open Settings, Accessibility, Vocal Shortcuts, and set it up.
  2. Choose the Hey Aura shortcut.
  3. Type Hey Aura as the phrase.
  4. Say it three times when asked.

The phone has to be unlocked. If you already have a Hey Aura phrase there, delete the old one first.
